# P1-05 — Later-Wave Hero Enhancement Trigger Pack (Blocked Spec)

## 1) Packet identity and purpose

P1-05 is a docs-only blocked-spec packet.

Its purpose is to formalize when later-wave hero enhancement overlays may legally enter production for Cultivation and Heart Law, and what those overlays are allowed to contain.

This packet does not authorize art generation, integration, cleanup, or scenic replacement.

## 2) Why this packet exists

Phase 1 documentation already treats hero enhancement families as later-wave work, but a standalone trigger packet is required so approvals cannot drift into speculative repaint requests.

P1-05 closes that governance gap by converting hero-kit deferral into explicit trigger law.

## 3) Covered blocked families

- Cultivation hero enhancement overlays
- Heart Law altar / seal support kit

Both families are explicitly blocked until trigger conditions are satisfied.

## 4) Non-negotiable blocked status

Until trigger conditions are fully satisfied, both families remain:

- `blocked for production`
- `blocked for live integration`
- `blocked for cleanup authority`

No packet may treat P1-05 as approval to repaint current hero/scenic owners.

## 5) Legal later-wave parts (allowlist only)

### Cultivation legal support parts

- altar base
- ring layers
- glow masks
- aura wisps
- doctrine-side underplates
- optional verse/stability companion support (only when shared chrome cannot carry it cleanly)

### Heart Law legal support parts

- altar base
- scripture stand / pedestal support
- seal / medallion companions
- circular doctrine overlays
- preview underlays
- resonance-emphasis support parts

## 6) Forbidden parts and authority boundaries

Forbidden regardless of trigger state:

- replacing cultivator / dantian center ownership
- replacing Heart Law core logic/content surfaces
- scenic repaint or screen-local background replacement
- giant hero-poster takeover overlays
- hidden cleanup authorization

Status hero art is not in scope for P1-05 and remains deferred unless a later packet explicitly reopens it with evidence.

## 7) Trigger logic (all conditions mandatory)

No legal production or integration can begin unless all are true:

1. Wave 0/additive screenshots already exist for the exact target screen.
2. Shared chrome for that screen is already mounted and approved.
3. A real missing support role still exists after shared chrome, overlays/masks, and shared FX are already present.
4. The missing-role statement is specific and screenshot-backed.
5. Section A cutover gate constraints are still being respected.
6. Hero art is not being used to hide unresolved layout or truth-surface problems.

If any one condition fails, status remains blocked.

## 8) Required proof bundle before unblock request

Every unblock request must provide:

- exact target screen id and family;
- screenshot set including base, truth states, high-fx, low-fx, reduced-motion;
- explicit evidence that current scenic owner remains primary;
- missing-role statement in support-role language;
- statement that request is additive and support-role only.

## 9) Review decision model (binary)

- `BLOCKED — TRIGGER NOT SATISFIED`
- `TRIGGER READY — LIMITED ART BRIEFING MAY BEGIN`

`TRIGGER READY` authorizes scoped briefing only for allowlisted parts; it does not authorize live integration by itself.

## 10) Proof that this packet is not repaint approval

- Existing Cultivation and Heart Law scenic/truth owners remain authoritative.
- P1-05 defines support overlays only.
- All trigger conditions prioritize missing-role proof and no-layout-shift truth.
- No destructive cleanup path is included.

## 11) Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Cultivation and Heart Law blocked families are both defined.
- [ ] Blocked state is explicit and enforceable.
- [ ] Legal allowlist parts are pre-declared.
- [ ] Trigger conditions include screenshot proof + shared-family prerequisite + cutover-gate safety.
- [ ] Packet cannot be read as repaint or integration approval.
- [ ] No binary art generation or live integration is implied.
