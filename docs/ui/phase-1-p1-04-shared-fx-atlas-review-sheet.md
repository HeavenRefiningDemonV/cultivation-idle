## 3) Red-flag checklist (auto-hold if any checked)

- [ ] Role behaves like a frame or plaque.
- [ ] Role is too bright, too loud, or too dominant for support atmosphere.
- [ ] Role reads as neon, sci-fi sweep, chromatic flicker, or confetti.
- [ ] Role is too screen-specific and not reusable.
- [ ] Role blocks readability or crowds truth surfaces.
- [ ] Role depends on continuous motion to make sense.
- [ ] Role implies or requires destructive cleanup.
