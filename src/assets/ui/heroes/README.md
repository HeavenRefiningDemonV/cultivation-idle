# Hero Asset Family

Hero assets are layered kits (not flattened poster art) for cultivation/heartlaw/world focal compositions.

## Belongs here
- Layered bases, rings, wisps, masks, and support indicators.
- Textless pieces designed for compositing and tinting.

## Does not belong here
- Screen-specific final composites.
- Text-bearing banners/labels.

## Naming expectations
- `cultivation_*`, `heartlaw_*`, `world_*` with explicit kit-part suffixes.
